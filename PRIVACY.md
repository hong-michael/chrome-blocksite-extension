# Focusgate privacy policy

_Last updated: November 10, 2026_

Focusgate is a website blocker that runs entirely in your browser.

## What Focusgate stores
On your device only, in Chrome's extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`):

- your block list, allow list, schedule and settings;
- the state of any focus session, pause or temporary access;
- a count of blocked visit attempts per website per day, kept for 30 days.

## What Focusgate does not do
- It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any data. The extension makes no network requests.
- It does not record your browsing history. It only looks at the address of the page you are
  navigating to, to decide whether to block it, and keeps no record of the pages it doesn't block.
- It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or third-party code.

## Permissions
Focusgate needs access to all websites so it can redirect blocked pages before they load and block
pages that are already open. It uses that access only to compare page addresses against your rules
on your device.

## Your control
You can export your data from **Settings → Backup & reset**, clear your statistics at any time, and
delete everything with **Reset everything** or by uninstalling the extension.
